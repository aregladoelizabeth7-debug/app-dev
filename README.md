## Tom and Jerry

**Tom and Jerry** is a classic animated series created by **William Hanna** and **Joseph Barbera**. It originally premiered on **February 10, 1940**, and quickly became one of the most iconic cartoons in history. The series focuses on the comedic rivalry between **Tom**, a cat, and **Jerry**, a mouse, who engage in a series of endless chases and pranks.

## Main Characters

#### Tom
- **Species**: Cat (Domestic)
- **Personality**: Mischievous, determined, often frustrated
- **Role**: Tom is often seen trying (and failing) to catch Jerry. Despite his efforts, he usually ends up being outsmarted or suffering various forms of physical comedy.
  
#### Jerry
- **Species**: Mouse
- **Personality**: Clever, quick-witted, and sometimes mischievous
- **Role**: Jerry is often portrayed as the smarter of the two, using his wit and agility to outsmart Tom and escape his grasp.
